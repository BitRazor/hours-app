Field craft

🌬️
Play the wind

Foxes smell you long before they see you. Keep the wind in your face and approach from downwind.

🌾
Edges beat wilderness

Best odds: where farmland, meadows and forest meet, low valleys, fjord shores and the outskirts of towns like Bergen, Stavanger, Ålesund, Molde and Førde. Mice and voles live in field margins.

🌅
Dusk and dawn

Be in position 30 min before sunset or 1 h before sunrise. Sit still for 45+ minutes. Movement gives you away, not shape.

❄️
Read the snow

In winter look for straight, single-file tracks (one print in front of the other). Follow them in the direction of travel, never to a den.

🔊
Listen in January–February

During mating season foxes scream and bark at night. Great for locating territories.

📷
Trail camera / thermal

Foxes are most active in full darkness. A trail cam on a path edge or a thermal scope finds them when your eyes can't.

Respect

🚫
Don't feed, don't crowd dens

Fed foxes become road-kill and problem animals. Watch cubs from 100 m+ with binoculars and leave if the adults get restless.

🦊
Arctic fox (fjellrev) is critically endangered

Fully protected. The purple zones are coarse 10–20 km mountain areas with records, not den sites. Never search for dens. Stay on trails near Finse and Hardangervidda.

🎯
Hunting season

Red fox can be hunted roughly mid-July to mid-April. Wear something visible in forest at dusk in that period.

How the map works

Chance index (0–100) is relative, not a probability. It blends:

• Habitat: elevation, slope and land cover share from a 150 m elevation model. Lowland and gentle terrain score high. Steep cliffs and high alpine terrain score low. Glaciers, bare summits above ~1650 m and open water score zero.
• Evidence: density of ~2000 red fox records from GBIF (Artsdatabanken/Artsobservasjoner and others), weighted toward the selected month.
• Season: in winter foxes follow prey down below the snow line; in summer they use higher ground too. Mating (Jan–Mar) and cub season (Jun–Jul) raise visibility.

Honest limits: sightings cluster where people live and walk, so remote valleys can be underrated. Lakes aren't masked. Treat it as a smart starting point, not a guarantee.
